Things To Do
Ashburn, Virginia is a suburb just outside of DC. It’s a place where drivers still look out for deer, neighborhoods are quiet, and grocery stores are the place to hang out. One of the best things about Ashburn is that within 30 minutes, you can either be in cities like Tysons or DC, or in the mountains of West Virginia.
Some of my favorite things to do in Ashburn are hanging out in One Loudoun or finding a great spot to watch planes fly through Dulles Airport. Skateboarding around the neighborhoods or going on walks through the trails is also nice. Ashburn is comforting and inviting, and though slightly boring, I wouldn’t want to leave.
